# Page notes

The detail behind [design-system.md](../design-system.md) 9a, 11, 12, 13a to 13j and 19 to 22, numbered the same so citations still work.

## Pages

13b. The trading agent, on the old Trade page that left the menu with decision 73: a run as a numbered timeline (done, working, skipped, failed, each with its model and cost), a ticket edged in `accent`, and Agent and Profile versions stamped on every call (decision 60).
13c. Reader: a Markdown file (profile.md, trade.md) opens full page, read-only, with Contents down the side and a 760px reading column; a "Label: value" line reads as a label and value, "Not set" in `dim` (decision 61).
13d. Profile: the plan as a road over time, not cards. A sentence headline carries the figures (where you are, the share of the goal, against the line); one chart shows the plan as a dashed line through each milestone to the goal, with a mint You dot today and each stop labelled with its date; below it a ledger of hairline rows: the plan's terms on one side, what's being built (BTC stack, cash floor, open items, the lesson) on the other, one column under 900px. Mint is you and done, `wait` next or short, `down` only the risk level. No pills, no middle dots. Figures round to K or M, with one decimal below 10K. Motion tells one story on load (the line draws, milestones arrive in turn, your dot lands, bars fill) and then answers change only: a live figure rolls in from the way it moved, tinted mint or red; what you open slides from where you opened it; an accepted diff folds away; none of it runs under reduced motion. Below 520px the ledger's labels sit above their values and the chart keeps only you, the next milestone and the goal. The agent tab is a profile sheet with Analyse's steps as a numbered list, the one real sequence. A proposed change shows as a line diff and saves only on Accept (decision 62).
13e. Dashboard: the same language as the Profile (13d). The day as a sentence, this month's deposits against the cap in one line (room left, days to the 1st), then the Weekly DCA section (13j), and nothing else for now: the week rail, Watch today, Ask the desk and the weekly recap are off until the desk's new agents arrive (decision 63, TODO 10).
13f. Portfolio: the same language. What you hold as a sentence, then the strip: the holdings by coin, 44px, the targets under it at 18px in the same order and colours; with targets set, a coin without one is neutral (`line-2`). Light slices take dark ink, deep ones light ink. Hover, focus or tap a slice for a sentence. Below, two hairline columns (Against your targets; Money in) and the holdings table without a card (decision 64).
13g. Settings: the same language. What the desk runs on as a sentence, then hairline sections with their status at the right; providers and models stay selectable cards, as they're choices (decision 65).
13h. Journal: the same language. Rules kept as a sentence (the count in mint), then the strip: a column a day, 12px dots stacked from the bottom, mint kept and `wait` broke a rule; then the ledger without a card and the tag lists last (decision 66).
13i. My agents: a sentence, then a week rota: a hairline row per agent with its name and purpose, a seven-day track (weekend tinted) with a 10px mint dot each time it wakes, the next one ringed, and a dashed now line through every row; the model on the right. Each agent has a face: an icon picked in its file (`- Icon:`, one of ten jobs, each in its own tone) with a corner dot for switched on, and a persona (`- Persona: title: traits`) shown under its name. A row opens in place to its persona, facts, its week in words and the file (decision 70).
13j. DCA book: on the Dashboard, one Weekly DCA section replaces the week rail (decision 72): dca-agent's icon and name, a sentence on where the week stands ("Next buy in 4 days: the week's USDC on 20 Nov (Fri) at 7pm."), then eight days on a line, Monday to Monday, with the buying window (Friday 7pm to Monday 7pm) as the one mint band, a white now line and each fill a dot at its time; under it the plan's coins with their amounts, and their limit or state once placed; one action at a time (Plan this week, Close out). Its own page (`#/dca-book`): a sentence, the totals, the plan's weeks as ticks (mint bought, amber a trim, `dim` missed, an outline this week), this week's planner, the positions with their lifelong IDs, and every order with filters and CSV export. Trims are `down`, buy-backs `up`, close-outs `wait`.

## Components and project rules

9a. Anything that opens over the page (a dialog, a sheet, a menu) closes on Esc and on a click outside it; a drag that starts inside doesn't close it. Dialogs get this from one handler in `main.tsx` (`dismiss.ts`); a confirm closed this way keeps, as its default.
11. Button (secondary on `panel-2`, primary on `accent`, buy on `up`, sell on `down`), Input with a unit on the right, segmented Buy/Sell, Tabs as pills with an icon beside the word, a filter as a rounded track whose choice fills in `accent`, Table, Tag (Buy, Sell, Buy now, Wait, Avoid), Pill (connection and kill switch), a Panel (a layer of light, not a card with a hairline), Dialog for the confirm step, Toast and Skeleton. Anything new is built from these (the recipe: [new-page.md](new-page.md)) and added here once settled.
12. One primary button per view: Analyse on Trade (then Use this order on the ticket), Create on a bot page.
13a. Rule-break banner (red, Keep to my rules first, Place anyway (logged) set apart), an Out of date tag (`wait`), and each agent's icon as its avatar (13i; decisions 58, 70).
19. Shell: the desk floats in a rounded frame (28px corners) over the field and its slow drift (decision 78); a 232px rail of its own, 22px corners, folds to a 64px icon strip, with Dashboard, Portfolio, Journal, Trade, Risk and Bots, then Profile and Settings, and at its foot the connections as quiet rows and the kill switch as a switch (red when off), always in view (spec 10). A slim header carries BTC, BTC dominance, market cap and Fear & Greed on the left (Fear & Greed opens its details), then the total (a link to the Portfolio) and the currency toggle. Details: [design-system/screens.md](screens.md#shell).
20. Screens: a sentence, then panels of layered light in place of widget cards and hairline bands (13d to 13f, decision 77); Bots in three tabs, Accumulation (the DCA book), Opportunities and Grid bots, each bot a block that opens its own page (decision 68, feature 92); Trade as the Trading account: money first, coins as cards, trades in tabs (decision 75). Destructive actions are red, set apart, and confirmed in a dialog whose default is to keep. Details: [design-system/screens.md](screens.md#screens).
21. Charts: the Trade page shows a compact 4-hour line with the agent's add zone, no-chase and invalidation drawn on it; Full chart opens TradingView's Advanced Chart in a dialog, in the dark theme on the desk's `bg` and `line` colours, framed from TradingView's own site (decision 22).
22. Status: Connected (dot, `up`), Stale, for data older than ten minutes (dot, `wait`), Disconnected (dot, `down`). Verdicts: Buy now (`up`), Wait (`wait`), Avoid (`down`). Smart trades keep their states from decision 12: Waiting for entry, Protected, Needs exits, Closed at take-profit, Closed at stop-loss and Cancelled. The kill switch: Trading on (`up`) or Trading off (`down`).
