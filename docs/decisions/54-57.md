# Decisions 54 to 57

The full records; [the index](../decisions.md) lists every decision in one line.

54. **The Portfolio page's Tax tab: lots in AUD, first in first out, from 1 Jul 2026, with the 12-month CGT countdown** (2026-11-15). Each buy order (keyed by pair and order id) since the cost start date is a lot at the day's ECB rate, before fees; anything held beyond those buys (older, moved in, the TAO wallet) is one lot of unknown age. It shows this financial year's realised gains and losses, the next lot to qualify, holdings under water before 30 June (with a wash-sale caution, no suggestion to sell) and, on the sell form, a warning when a sale uses a lot under 12 months. Estimates for records, not tax advice; today's rule, not the proposed 2027 change. Why: the competitor research's biggest lever for an Australian holder (CoinTracker, Syla, Koinly). Instead of: full history and hand-entered lots, which the owner turned down for now.
55. **Drift from the Profile's target allocation, outside a band (default ±5 points, on the Profile), is flagged on Portfolio, with a link that prefills a limit order back toward target within the per-trade size** (2026-11-15). Nothing is sent; the owner confirms on the Trade page. Why: turns "should I buy?" into a rule (Shrimpy, Pionex). Instead of: flag only, or automatic rebalancing.
56. **A Journal page of every filled trade the desk can see, one entry per order, with where it came from (ST-, DCA-, TR-, GRID- or the order form or Binance app), its realised result, automatic rule checks and the owner's setup and mistake tags; a 30-day score of rules kept** (2026-11-15). The checks: a buy over the per-trade size, a coin over its cap as it stands now, deposits over the monthly cap that month. Coins read are those held, watched or traded by the desk. Why: makes the Profile's rules measurable (Edgewonk, TraderSync). Instead of: the desk's trades only.
57. **A morning summary on the owner's phone through their own Telegram bot** (2026-11-15). The bot token is saved from Settings to `.env` like the AI key (decision 51); the chat is linked only by a one-time code the owner sends the bot from Settings (bot names are public, so a stranger's message is never taken), and kept; one message a day at a set Sydney time with the coach's mood line, key points and deposits left, sent by the watcher, tried once a day. Telegram sees those figures, never keys. Credentials saved from Settings are reloaded from the mounted `.env` at start-up. Why: the owner chose the summary only, over alerts and fills. Instead of: price alerts or fills by Telegram, for now.

The Plan page is renamed Profile and sits above Settings in the side panel (the owner's request, 15 Nov 2026); decision 52 still describes it.
